# Coga marketing plan

**Owner direction, 2026-09-10:** first reference and group the marketing
materials, then start afresh using the previous work as inspiration.

Start with the [grouped catalogue](../map/SKILL.md). It covers the existing
ideas, research, drafts, evidence, channels, writing methods and public
surfaces. The previous three-essay campaign is preserved in
[launch history](../launch-history/SKILL.md). Its post count, angles,
strategic fork, titles, channel sequence, scorecard and launch gates are
prior choices to consider again. No replacement campaign has been chosen.

## First: review the catalogue

The material is grouped by subject:

1. [Positioning and strategy](../map/SKILL.md#positioning-and-strategy).
2. [Previous campaigns and drafts](../map/SKILL.md#previous-campaigns-and-drafts).
3. [Evidence and examples](../map/SKILL.md#evidence-and-examples).
4. [Distribution and audience](../map/SKILL.md#distribution-and-audience).
5. [Writing methods](../map/SKILL.md#writing-methods).
6. [Public surfaces and launch dependencies](../map/SKILL.md#public-surfaces-and-launch-dependencies).

Each entry says where the material lives and how to use it: current product
reference, dated observation, previous proposal, future work or excluded
source. The catalogue also names the related ownership and housekeeping
tickets. Reviewing that inventory is the current deliverable; the work below
starts from it after the owner accepts the grouping.

## Then: make a fresh plan

| Order | Work and concrete output | Owner / existing ticket |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Decide whom to reach, what they should understand or try, and what outcome matters. Develop distinct story/example options, recommend one, and record the owner's choice with reasons and exclusions. Use existing ideas as inspiration and make new examples where useful. | Agent prepares; `nicktoper` decides in [story and examples](../../../tasks/marketing/plan/collect-public-examples-for-the-launch.md). |
| 2 | Write a one-sentence pitch, short expansion and narrative outline from the chosen story. Identify authored illustrations and link factual claims to support. Record the reviewed message in positioning. | Agent prepares; `nicktoper` reviews in [pitch and narrative](../../../tasks/marketing/plan/write-the-pitch-and-narrative.md). |
| 3 | Choose the deliverables, formats, channels, order, necessary product/readiness work and audience measurement. Give each selected item an owner and completion condition; explicitly keep, change, defer or drop the old ideas. | `nicktoper` owns the decision in [build the launch plan](../../../tasks/marketing/build-the-launch-plan.md), with agent support. |
| 4 | Rewrite or create execution briefs for only the selected work, including an appropriate workflow and owner review before publication. Then produce that work. | Each selected ticket's assignee, after the campaign decision. |

Story selection precedes final copy. Copy informs the campaign choice.
A catalogue entry or an existing draft does not select that item for the
campaign. The three essay tickets, README revision, community proposal and
older domain idea are available inputs; their existence sets no new launch
prerequisite. Product fixes continue to be governed by their own tickets.

## Decisions still open

- Audience, desired reader action and success objective.
- Central story, worked examples, message and tone.
- Whether to use essays, a product announcement, a demonstration, another
  format or some combination; how many pieces and in what order.
- Channels and their timing, including the earlier blog, Bookface, HN,
  Lobsters, Reddit, fastjvm.com and YC proposals.
- Which release, install, documentation or community work the chosen reader
  path actually requires.
- Audience baselines, checkpoints, success criteria and response to a miss.

The prior answers are linked in the catalogue. The owner makes these
decisions in the tickets above; inventory work does not make them implicitly.

## Source and publication boundaries

Product purpose remains in [vision](../../../../docs/vision.md), and product
behavior in the relevant Coga contracts. A new message must describe the
product accurately. [Positioning](../positioning/SKILL.md) records source
limits and where the future reviewed message will land.

Examples may be newly authored illustrations or public demonstrations.
Identify their form. Claims about an observed event need public support;
a context appearing in a prompt alone does not demonstrate successful reuse.
Exclude all private-repo narrative quotations. No measured productivity
multiplier or unsupported efficiency claim belongs in the new copy.

The token/time experiment remains dropped under the owner's 2026-09-09
decision. Audience measurement remains part of planning; its prior numeric
targets and day-14 gate are historical, not defaults for the new campaign.
[Distribution](../distribution/SKILL.md) preserves dated account observations
and the measurement limits; applicable rules and facts need checking when a
channel is selected.

External publication and personal-account actions remain owner actions.
The [writing skill](../../../skills/marketing/write-post/SKILL.md) applies to
an explicitly selected and briefed post after the new choices are recorded.
