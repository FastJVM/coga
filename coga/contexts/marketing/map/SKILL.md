first deliverable: locate and group the previous work so new marketing
decisions can draw on it.

**Owner direction, 2026-09-10:** start afresh after reviewing this inventory.

wording does not select a format, post count, message, channel or launch gate
for the new plan. Current product contracts and source restrictions still
apply; inclusion in this catalogue does not approve a claim for publication.

Coverage checked against the repo on 2026-09-10: the dedicated marketing
contexts and reference files, marketing writing skill, every current ticket
and attachment under `coga/tasks/marketing/`, and the related sources below.

that only mention a launch or this repo's name are not marketing sources.
Third-party writing references are indexed as a library, not Coga copy.
This is a repo inventory, not a fresh audit of live accounts or websites.
