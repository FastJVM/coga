### Claim and wording limits

Carried forward from the retired positioning context (owner decisions,
2026-10-13 and 2026-10-15). Apply these to the piece, its title and replies:

- Do not position Coga as an audit, governance or compliance tool or use those
  words. Avoid novelty/priority claims ("first", "only", "new", "unique"),
  shared-knowledge category slogans ("knowledge compounds", "learn once",
  "multiplayer", "company brain"), "human at the center", "human-centered",
  "human-in-the-loop", and "progressive disclosure". The design principles
  may express human purpose and compounding improvements; this is not a
  measured marketing result or an exception for the piece.
- Corrections persist through reviewed file edits, not automatic learning.
  Human reading is chosen per PR; Coga does not force review of every ticket.
  Code is "cheap enough to throw away", not free. Keep setup, self-support
  and actual execution limits visible; do not promise managed autonomy.
- Support observed events with public tickets, diffs, PRs or demonstrations.
  Label other examples as illustrations. Prompt inclusion proves delivery;
  successful reuse needs evidence from later work. No invented results,
  productivity multiplier, or unsupported token/time saving.
- Private-repo narrative quotations remain excluded. Do not recover or
  re-collect the deleted `phase-0-audit/narrative-candidates.md` attachment.
  Do not describe an operator beyond the public record. Competitor claims
  need dated, verified support, not assertions about "nobody else".
- Archive links do not authorize archive reading. Use current public evidence
  unless the human explicitly requests archived material.
