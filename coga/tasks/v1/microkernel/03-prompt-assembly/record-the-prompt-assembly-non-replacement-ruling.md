### Decision to record

- C1 violated the checkout gate: in owner-assist it merged `origin/main` into the prepared branch without a request. It also lost a successful run in that case. The predeclared protocol makes either disqualifying.
- C1's authoring advantage (2/2 vs 1/2) did not survive the gate and did not come from the predicted execute/bump conflict: the baseline failure was a missing cold evaluator, and no baseline authoring run advanced the ticket.
- Secondary measures showed no gain: inspectability +1/6 (threshold +2), payload −3.40% (threshold 15%). No simplification was claimed.
- Ticket 03 (`03-implement-the-proven-prompt-assembly`) is not eligible and stays unlaunched.

### Reopening conditions

- A materially revised candidate needs a new owner-approved protocol and a new paired comparison; ticket 02's evidence does not carry over.
- A revision should keep authoring free of execution-loop text without weakening the owner-assist/reconciliation guidance that keeps a read-only assist from merging into a prepared branch.
- Evidence: `coga/tasks/v1/microkernel/03-prompt-assembly/02-compare-current-and-candidate-prompt-assembly/report.md` (§3, §4, §8) and `blind/verdicts.csv`.
